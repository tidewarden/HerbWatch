Feral Druid Dungeon Drops

Dungeons ranked by how many items they drop from the WoW Forever Feral (cat) and Feral (bear) lists on wowtbc.gg. An item on more than one list counts once.

Level cap

The source lists are not ranked. wowtbc.gg lists up to ten useful items per slot, in no order, so a count here means "upgrades worth a look", not strict best in slot.

Drop sources come from wowtbc.gg, and from the ForeverDungeonJournal addon's loot tables where wowtbc.gg gives none. Dungeons tagged "Forever" are new in WoW Forever.
