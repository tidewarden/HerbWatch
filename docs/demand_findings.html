Background, written Nov 12

Where the market stood on Nov 12

Prices are still falling and nobody is raiding yet. Between the 00:22 and 13:03 snapshots on Nov 12, 47% of about 1,860 actively traded Alliance items fell and 18% rose, with a median change of −3.4%.

  • No raid demand yet. No flasks, protection potions or Greater Arcane Elixirs are listed on either faction. Major Mana Potion sells for about 1g, below its 1g 07s material cost.

  • Raids reportedly open Dec 9: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair. The date comes from pre-launch coverage and was not confirmed on a primary page. Molten Core is reported as later, with no date.

  • Herbs fell all day. Dreamfoil went from 60s to 14s 75c in about 26 hours, and Plaguebloom from 80s 87c to 35s 98c. A bounce at 13:03 faded by 15:22.

  • More level 60s add supply faster than demand. New 60s mostly farm and sell. The buyers of top-level herbs are raiders, and they are not buying yet.

  • Gold is scarce. Players are saving for mounts and training, which pushes prices down across the board.

  • Horde prices fell toward Alliance levels during the day, so most of the early "cheap versus Horde" gaps were Horde outliers and not Alliance bargains.

Waylaid Crates. This is a repeatable supply turn-in for Merchant's Favor, open to everyone. Artisan crates take Runecloth, Thick or Rugged Leather, Thorium and Dark Iron ore and bars, Dreamfoil, Icecap, Plaguebloom, Mountain Silversage, Dream Dust, Brilliant Shards and Thorium Widgets. The Pack Kodo reward costs 6,000 Favor, which means hundreds of crates. Quantities per crate come from secondary guides.

New raid consumables.

  • Four new flasks work only in Mount Hyjal, Hyjal Summit and Barrow Deeps. Each takes 1 Death Lotus plus 12–18 Demonsage or Marefoil.

  • Classic flasks still take 30 herbs plus 1 Black Lotus.

  • Elixirs last 30 minutes, so a raider uses several per night.

  • New potions include Major Frenzy, Major Spellblasting and Major Mender's. New elixirs include Grizzly, Ferocity, Cunning, Phalanx, Owl and Whale.

  • All consumable recipes checked matched the recipe table in the local game files.

New enchants.

  • Two-hand Mighty Spell Power and Mighty Healing Power use Fel Crystal, Torbernite, Azerothium Bar, 4 Nexus Crystal and 6 Greater Eternal Essence.

  • Gloves Lotus Claw uses 1 Death Lotus.

  • Two-hand Strength +25 uses 8 Large Brilliant Shard, 10 Greater Eternal Essence, 8 Illusion Dust and 4 Arcane Crystal.

Level 60 gear. BiS lists for all 29 specs are mostly crafted, dungeon and reputation gear. Of 209 crafted BiS items, 16 need raid-only materials. Those were counted at a quarter weight, because only raiders will make them.

Class mounts. The paladin Charger takes 6 Arcanite Bar, 40 Runecloth, 10 Arthas' Tears, a Pristine Black Diamond and an Azerothian Diamond. The warlock Dreadsteed takes 35 Black Dragonscale, 25 Dark Iron Ore, 3 Arcanite Bar and 6 Large Brilliant Shard.

How the numbers were built, and what is uncertain

  • Supply is the Alliance auction count in three snapshots on Nov 12 (00:22, 13:03, 15:22), plus how each material is obtained.

  • Auction counts are stacks, not units, and total listings fell 32% between 00:22 and 13:03, partly from overnight auctions expiring.

  • One day of data is not a trend. The 13:03 herb bounce reversed within two hours.

  • The Dec 9 raid date and raid sizes come from pre-launch coverage with conflicting details.

  • Sources for Demonsage, Marefoil, Death Lotus, Fel Leather, Fel Crystal and Heavy Thorium Ore are not confirmed anywhere the research could read.

  • Waylaid Crate quantities, reputation turn-ins and libram costs are from secondary sites or Classic assumptions.

  • BootyBayBroker sends no price history for this realm, so there is no 7-day average from the site; HerbWatch builds its own.
