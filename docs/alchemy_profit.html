Most in-demand raid herbs

Herbs for one raid night by one player of every spec: one flask, one of each recommended elixir and 8 of each recommended combat potion (the best flask, elixirs and potion for each spec's stat weights; elixirs stack unless they give the same stat, one combat potion per boss). Demand is a relative weight, not units sold. Price = cheapest auction at the newest reading (TSM hourly, or your in-game check if newer). Buy now?
