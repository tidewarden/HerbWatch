Best leads for you

For a level 60 warrior with Mining and Alchemy, solo in the open world. Prices are live; drop chances from Wowhead where we have the page, otherwise VMaNGOS.

Transmute: Arcanite
32g 45s per transmute

Arcanite Bar sells for 46g 0s; Arcane Crystal 11g 0s + Thorium Bar 24s. You have Alchemy and Mining: mined crystals make it nearly all profit. About 2-day cooldown.

Mine Heavy Thorium Veins, Mount Hyjal
Fel Crystal 14g 22s

Torbernite 2g 81s. Guides say both come from Heavy Thorium Veins in Hyjal (rare, fel-smoke Rich Thorium look); others say the source is unknown, so confirm in game. Fel Crystal is the Legionite transmute input.

Recipe farm: Greater Nature Protection
1g 25s a kill from the recipe

Recipe: Greater Nature Protection Potion 38g 0s, drop 3.30% (Wowhead). Rotting Behemoth / Decaying Horror, Western Plaguelands, 55–57, not elite. You can also learn it: the potion has none listed before the raids.

Recipe farm: Greater Shadow Protection
91s a kill from the recipe

Recipe: Greater Shadow Protection Potion 55g 90s, drop 1.64% (Wowhead). Dark Adept / Shadowmage, Eastern Plaguelands (Northdale area), 58–60, not elite.

Crusader formula
1g 4s a kill from the recipe

Formula: Enchant Weapon - Crusader 250g 99s, drop 0.42% (Wowhead). Scarlet Spellbinder, Western Plaguelands, 57–58, not elite. A lottery: about 1 in 240 kills at Wowhead's rate, and it rarely sells, so it is a bonus rather than income.

Elemental lieutenants (when they are up)
one kill pays an essence or more

The Windreaver (Silithus) 76g 99s; Princess Tempestria (Winterspring) 44g 65s; Baron Charr (Un'Goro Crater) 6g 53s; Avalanchion (Azshara) 3g 82s. Single named elites with big essence drops; check whether they spawn in Forever.
