Net each is the lowest crate listing plus the cheapest fill at lowest listings, minus the silver the turn-in pays back. Net per Favor divides that by the Favor the crate gives, so the cheapest Favor is at the top. Net total runs from every item at its lowest listing up to every item at its market value: a big buy uses up the cheap listings, so the real cost lands between the two.
