Net each is the lowest crate listing plus the cheapest fill at lowest listings, minus the silver the turn-in pays back. Net per Favor divides that by the Favor the crate gives, so the cheapest Favor is at the top. Net total runs from every item at its lowest listing up to every item at its market value: a big buy uses up the cheap listings, so the real cost lands between the two. Change the rep or Favor box to see crates needed and the whole plan for another amount.

Favor and silver back per crate, as reported from the game: Apprentice 5 Favor and 2s 50c, Journeyman 10 and 5s, Expert 15 and 7s 50c, Artisan 20 and 10s. Green (uncommon) crates, which are Fabrics, Ingots and Parts, give double of both.
