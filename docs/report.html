Buy out the market

Tracked items with only a few up for sale on our auction house. Checked in game = the HerbWatchAH addon looked at the auction house (press Check my items there, then Reload to save): the exact number for sale and the exact cost of buying every one. Otherwise it is an estimate: TSM only gives the number of auctions, not how many items each holds, so a stack counts as one and the real cost can be far higher. Normal price = the middle of its 14-day value, 60-day average and the US average sale. Your price = what you would relist at: type any price, the profit updates (it starts at the normal price; what you type is remembered in this browser). Profit is after the 5% auction cut. Days to sell = how many ÷ what one auction house sells a day (TSM, US average, undercounts).
